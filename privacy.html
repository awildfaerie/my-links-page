Privacy Policy

Analytics and tracking

This site does not use analytics or tracking to monitor visitors, build profiles, or measure browsing behavior.

Server requests

When you visit this site, Nginx receives the information your browser sends with its request so it can deliver the page. This can include your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request, and browser-provided details such as your user agent or referrer.

The site does not collect additional visitor information. Any request details retained in server logs depend on the server configuration and are used for operating and troubleshooting the site, not for analytics.

External links

This page links to third-party websites and services. When you follow a link, that service may collect information under its own privacy policy; this site does not control those practices.
